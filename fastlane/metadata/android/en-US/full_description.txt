T2DECODE is a fully offline, air-gapped learning platform designed for IT enthusiasts, cybersecurity students, and network administrators. 

Features:
- Completely offline: No internet connection required, no analytics, no tracking.
- Interactive Labs: Network, System, Cloud, and Cryptography simulators.
- Local AI Assistant: Integrate with your local Ollama models (Phi-3, Llama 3) for an air-gapped AI tutor.
- P2P Communication: GhostLink feature allows local LAN encrypted communication without servers.
- Offline Tools: Built-in utilities (Subnet calculators, Hash generators, CRON helpers, and more).
- Comprehensive Courses: Learn at your own pace with markdown-based courses and quizzes.
